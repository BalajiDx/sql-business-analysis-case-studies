# SQL Business Analysis Case Studies

Twenty-five business questions answered with SQL on two small schemas, one **banking** (branches, customers, accounts,
transactions) and one **retail** (stores, products, customers, orders). Each script states the question, uses the
technique a business analyst would reach for, and its output is saved next to it.

> **Synthetic data.** Every row was generated by `01. Dataset/generate_dataset.py` (seed 7) for learning. No real bank,
> retailer or customer data is used. Findings describe this dataset only.

## What it shows
- Joins across 3-4 tables, CTEs and sub-queries
- Window functions: `RANK`, `ROW_NUMBER`, `NTILE`, `LAG`, running totals and `SUM() OVER (PARTITION BY)`
- Cohort and retention analysis, RFM segmentation, Pareto (80/20) analysis, basket analysis
- KPI design: AOV, margin %, repeat rate, dormancy, channel mix, year-on-year growth
- Writing the business answer, not just the query (`03. Case Study Notes/`)

## The 25 questions
| # | Question | Techniques |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | How many customers and accounts does each branch hold? | JOIN, GROUP BY, COUNT DISTINCT |
| 02 | How many products does each customer hold, and how many hold only a savings account? | CTE, conditional aggregation |
| 03 | How do monthly transaction count and value move through 2024? | strftime, GROUP BY, LAG |
| 04 | What share of transactions goes through each channel, by quarter? | CASE quarter, SUM() OVER (PARTITION BY) |
| 05 | Which 10 customers brought in the most net money (credits minus debits) in 2024? | JOIN, conditional SUM, RANK |
| 06 | Within each region, how do branches rank on transaction value? | RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY), share of region |
| 07 | Which open savings/current accounts had no transaction in the last 180 days of 2024? | LEFT JOIN, MAX date, julianday |
| 08 | What share of customers in each segment took out more than they put in? | CTE, HAVING-style filter, AVG of flags |
| 09 | How often do accounts transact? Median-style view of the gap between transactions by account type. | LAG over dates, julianday, bucketing |
| 10 | For each opening year, how many accounts were opened and what share has since closed? | cohort by year, conditional aggregation |
| 11 | Which transactions are more than 5x the account's own average (possible review cases)? | AVG() OVER (PARTITION BY), ratio filter, ranking |
| 12 | How do the customer segments compare on activity, ticket size and digital adoption? | multi-table JOIN, KPI ratios |
| 13 | What are monthly net revenue, delivered orders and average order value? | JOIN, GROUP BY month, derived ratio |
| 14 | Which categories earn the most revenue and gross margin? | JOIN, margin calculation, share of total |
| 15 | What are the top 3 products by revenue in each category? | ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY ... ORDER BY) |
| 16 | Which stores beat the company average on revenue per order and margin? | CTE, comparison with AVG() OVER () |
| 17 | Of customers who first bought in each quarter, what share bought again in the following quarters? | cohort CTEs, quarter index arithmetic, pivot with conditional aggregation |
| 18 | What share of customers order a second time, and how long do they take? | ROW_NUMBER, LAG, julianday |
| 19 | How do customers split into RFM segments (recency, frequency, monetary)? | NTILE(4), CTEs, CASE segment rules |
| 20 | Which stores have the highest return and cancellation rates? | conditional aggregation, ratio, ORDER BY |
| 21 | How much margin do discounts cost, and do discounted lines sell more units? | GROUP BY discount level, margin math |
| 22 | What share of revenue comes from the top 10%, 20% and 50% of customers? | cumulative SUM() OVER, ROW_NUMBER, percentile cut-offs |
| 23 | Which pairs of categories most often appear in the same basket? | self-join on OrderID, de-duplicated pairs |
| 24 | Do Gold and Silver loyalty members spend and visit more than non-members? | JOIN, per-customer CTE, averages by tier |
| 25 | How did each store's revenue change from 2023 to 2024? | pivot with conditional SUM, growth %, NULLIF |

## How to run
```bash
cd "01. Dataset" && python generate_dataset.py && cd ..     # optional: the data is already included
sqlite3 case_studies.db < "02. SQL Scripts/00_Create_Tables_And_Load.sql"
sqlite3 -header -column case_studies.db < "02. SQL Scripts/19_RFM_Segmentation.sql"
```
Scripts are written for **SQLite 3.25+** (window functions). Script 00 lists the SQL Server / MySQL equivalents for the
date functions used. The generator needs Python 3 with `pandas` and `numpy`.
